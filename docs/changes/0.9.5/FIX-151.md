# FIX-151: Findings name the records that settle them

## docs/operator-guide.md

### In "5. The work loop: order, brief, assignment, attempt, result", replace the last sentence of the paragraph beginning "An output's `location` is a repository-relative path"

> Results already in the estate remain readable. From 0.9.5 (FIX-151), the 0.9.2 upgrade checkpoint and the counting checkpoints it
> follows can establish that a result's exact bytes were present when the rule began. An estate that first adopted signed history
> at that upgrade uses its adoption checkpoint as this witness. The finding names the migration, the checkpoint at the rule's
> start and the checkpoint that witnessed the result. A result without that evidence still carries the warning, and a new result
> with a URL scheme in an output location is refused.

### In "12. Reading the estate", after the paragraph beginning "`vericairn progress` is the report"

> From 0.9.5 (FIX-151), the estate check reports a stale subject as information when a decision answers its ask, an ask records its
> withdrawal, a record closes its operator request, an operation settles its agent run, an attempt continues work on a successor
> assignment, or an acceptance settles the current goal or work order. The finding names the settling record. Required attention
> also reads a settled run's incident as information when an acceptance settles its work order or a successor replaces its assignment.
> A refused checked change's incident becomes information when a later successful operation names the refusal in its reconciliation.
> The remaining stale subjects and incidents keep their original findings.
