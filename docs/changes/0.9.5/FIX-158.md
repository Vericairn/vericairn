# FIX-158: Check a record's body before asking for a signature

## docs/operator-guide.md

### In "Presence prompts, announced and counted (0.9.3)", after the paragraph beginning "Up to 0.9.3 a device"

> From 0.9.5 (FIX-158), a single-record write such as `records new` or `work assign` checks the body's fields, types, lengths and
> record-specific rules, the subject rules the record alone decides (whether it has one, its type and its pinned hash) and its own
> bytes with the estate check's content scan, before asking the signing device. Such an error is reported without a presence
> prompt. After signing, the runtime checks references and rules that depend on other records or signatures against the current
> estate under the write lock, before storing the record. For a batch write, the content scan under that lock reports the
> findings from all its proposed records together.
