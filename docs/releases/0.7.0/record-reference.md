Generated by the build tooling (`vericairn-docs generate`) from the record type registry of build 0.7.0-dev+5dbc5022ae4878bc53e7818d4d788cd1a739aeff on 2026-10-16. ## `acceptance`

Acceptance of an exact subject revision: a work order, or a result toward one; a goal; or another record, such as a release candidate, which only the operator accepts. Milestones and their results need the operator; ordinary work may be self-accepted only under an explicit authority scope. A goal is accepted by its sponsor when the sponsor is a person and by an operator when an agent sponsors it, after its sponsor activated it, relying on its current evaluation, which finds every condition met, and never by that evaluation's author or by the author of one of its steps.

Subject: required, pinned to an exact hash.

| Field | Kind | Required | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `statement` | `string` | required | What is accepted and on what basis. |
| `basis` | `ulid[]` | required | Review records of the same subject that the acceptance relies on; may be empty for operator acceptance. A goal's acceptance names the goal's current evaluation. |
| `self` | `boolean` | required | True when the accepting principal authored the subject; must match the facts. |
